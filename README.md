# CIS UI/UX Roadmap
UI / UX Roadmap 2026

Path: Entry > Beginner > Intermediate > Advanced



Level : Beginner (Week 1 - 9)

UX

week 1: UX vs UI vs Product Design
- Think: Who is this for and what problem do they have?
- Task: Post on LinkedIn explaining the difference with a real app


Week 2: Design Thinking and Empathize
- Think: What do I know vs assume about the user?
- Task: Choose a project idea and list 10 assumptions about its users.

Week 3: User Research & Personas and Problem Statements
- Think: What is the cheapest way to learn the truth?
         What is the one problem worth solving?
- Task: Write an interview guide and interview 3 people.
Make an affinity map, 2 personas, and 3 "How Might We" questions.


Week 4: User Flows and IA & Wireframes
- Think: What is the shortest path to the user's goal?
- Task: Draw the main user flow and a sitemap.

UI
Week 5: What makes an interface good
-Task: Pick 3 apps. Write one good and one bad UI point for each.

Week 6: Color Theory & Typography

-Task: Make 3 palettes (fintech, kids, wellness) and explain each.
-Build a type scale with 2 font pairings, English and Arabic.

Week 7: Figma basics & Grids

Task: Recreate a business card and a social media post.
-Set up a 12-column web grid and a 4-column mobile grid.


Week 8: Spacing, layout, contrast & components 

-Task: Fix a messy screen and show before and after.

week 9 : first mini project 

# Intermediate (Week 10 - 18)

UX

Week 1: Empathy and Journey Maps
- Think: Where is the user's biggest pain?
- Task: Build a journey map with stages, actions, emotions, opportunities.

Week 2: Competitor Analysis and UX Audit
- Think: Where is the gap competitors leave?
- Task: Audit 3 competitors in a comparison table.

Week 3: Accessibility & Interaction and UX Writing
- Think: Who could this design exclude?
What must the user know right now, in the fewest words?
- Task: Check your project against WCAG and fix 5 issues.
Rewrite errors, empty states, and button labels in English and Arabic.

Week 4: Prototyping Strategy & Usability Test Planning
- Think: What is the smallest prototype that answers my biggest question?
What would prove my design wrong?
- Task: Write 3 key tasks users must complete.
Write a test plan with goals, 3 tasks, script, and success criteria.
.

         UI

Week 5: Auto-Layout & Responsive Design
- Task: Build a navbar, card list, and form that resize properly.
Design one landing page for desktop, tablet, and mobile.


Week 6: Design System Foundations & Components and Variants
- Task: Build a foundations page (colors, type, spacing, shadows).
Build button states and input states.


Week 7: Prototyping in Figma & AI Tools
- Task: Build a clickable prototype for the 3 key tasks.

Week 8: Dashboards
- Task: Design a dashboard with 2 chart types, a table, and filters.


---

Advanced (Week 19 - 28)

         UX

Week 1: UX Metrics
- Think: How will I know it worked?
- Task: Define 4 metrics and how to measure each.

Week 2: Case Study Storytelling
- Think: Can a recruiter understand my process in 3 minutes?
- Task: Write a first case study draft from your Week 10 project.

Week 3: Product Thinking
- Think: What would I cut with half the time?
- Task: Prioritize features with RICE or MoSCoW and define an MVP.


Week 4: Portfolio and Career
- Think: What story should a hiring manager remember?
- Task: Finalize 2 case studies, one solo and one team.

          UI

Week 5: Variables and Light/Dark Modes
- Task: Add light and dark modes with variables.

Week 6: Localization and RTL
- Task: Convert one flow from English to Arabic RTL.

Week 7: Capstone Visual Direction
- Task (team): Mood board, style tile, design system foundation.

Week 8: High-Fidelity Capstone
- Task: High-fidelity prototype with light/dark or Arabic/English.

Week 9: Portfolio Design
- Task: Publish a portfolio with 2 projects and an About  every decision yours
